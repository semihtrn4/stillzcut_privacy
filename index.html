Privacy Policy — AI Haircut Style App
Last updated: April 1, 2026

This privacy notice for Stilzcut — AI Haircut Style App ("we," "us," or "our") explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Stilzcut helps you discover, visualize, and explore haircut styles powered by AI — designed for anyone looking to find their perfect look before stepping into the salon.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the app.

00 / Summary of Key Points

  • We collect device identifiers and purchase data primarily through RevenueCat to manage your subscription.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties or use it for advertising.
  • Photos you use for AI style try-ons are processed on-device or via temporary server inference and are never stored on our servers.
  • All haircut visualizations and AI results are saved locally on your device.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from users under 18 years of age.

01 / What Information Do We Collect?

Data Collected Automatically

  • Device ID — Used by RevenueCat to identify your device for subscription management.
  • User ID — A RevenueCat-assigned identifier to link your account with your subscription status.
  • Purchase & Subscription History — Records of your Stilzcut Pro subscription processed via Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Product Interaction Data — Anonymized data about how you use the app (e.g., styles browsed, AI features used) to improve the platform.
  • Crash & Performance Data — Technical diagnostics including crash logs, launch times, and performance metrics to ensure app stability.

Data You Provide

  • Stilzcut does not require account registration or login.
  • If you contact our support team, we may collect your email address and the content of your message solely to respond to your inquiry.

Photos & AI Processing

📸 Photos you import for AI haircut try-on are used only to generate your style preview. They are processed ephemerally and are NEVER stored on our servers after your result is delivered.

✂️ All AI-generated haircut visualizations and saved looks are stored LOCALLY on your device. We do not retain any facial or image data beyond the duration of active processing.

Camera & Photo Library Access

Stilzcut may request access to your camera and photo library to let you take or import photos for AI haircut visualization. These permissions are used exclusively for the core functionality of the app. You may revoke them at any time in your device Settings.

Payment Information

All payments are processed directly through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not have access to your payment card details, bank information, or billing address. RevenueCat receives only confirmation of your subscription status.

03 / How Do We Use Your Information?

  • To verify and manage your Stilzcut Pro subscription status
  • To unlock premium styles, AI features, and tools for Pro subscribers
  • To improve app performance and fix technical issues
  • To analyze aggregate usage patterns and expand our style catalog
  • To respond to customer support inquiries
  • To comply with legal obligations

04 / Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services that may process data on our behalf:

  • RevenueCat — Subscription and in-app purchase management. Collects Device ID, User ID, and purchase history. RevenueCat Privacy Policy →
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — Payment processing and subscription verification.

We do not integrate advertising networks, cross-app analytics SDKs, or data brokers.

05 / Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Turkey, we process your data based on:

  • Performance of a contract — To deliver your Stilzcut Pro subscription
  • Legitimate interests — To improve app stability and performance
  • Legal obligations — To comply with applicable laws
  • Your consent — Where explicitly required (e.g., camera and photo library access)

06 / When Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — RevenueCat, as described above, to manage subscriptions
  • Legal requirements — When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business transfers — In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • Fraud prevention — To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions

07 / Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Subscription records managed by RevenueCat are retained in accordance with their data retention policy. Anonymized usage and diagnostic data may be retained for up to 12 months. AI-processed images are not retained beyond the active inference session.

08 / How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

09 / Do We Collect Data from Minors?

Stilzcut is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

10 / Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (including revoking camera / photo library permissions via device Settings)
  • Data portability (receive your data in a structured format)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

11 / Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us at the email address below. We will process your request within 30 days. Note that some data may need to be retained for legal compliance purposes.

12 / Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Stilzcut mobile app does not use browser cookies. Standard mobile identifiers (Device ID) are used solely for subscription management as described above. We do not use cross-app tracking technologies or advertising identifiers (IDFA) for ad targeting.

13 / Do-Not-Track

As Stilzcut does not engage in cross-site tracking, Do-Not-Track signals are not applicable to our services.

14 / US Privacy Rights

If you are a US resident, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. We do not sell personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or similar state laws.

15 / Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
